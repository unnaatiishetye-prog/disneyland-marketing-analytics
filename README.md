# 🎢 Disneyland Marketing Campaign Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Disneyland marketing campaign performance using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into ticket revenue, advertising expenditure, ticket sales, monthly revenue trends, marketing channel performance, and Return on Advertising Spend (ROAS).

The objective is to identify effective marketing channels and campaigns, understand revenue trends, and support data-driven marketing decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze total ticket revenue and ticket sales.
- Monitor total advertising expenditure.
- Compare marketing channel performance.
- Track monthly ticket revenue trends.
- Evaluate campaign effectiveness using ROAS.
- Identify opportunities to improve marketing performance.

## Dashboard KPIs

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Ticket Revenue | Total revenue generated from ticket sales |
| Total Ad Spend | Total advertising expenditure |
| Total Tickets Sold | Number of tickets sold |
| ROAS | Return on advertising spend |
| Monthly Ticket Revenue | Revenue trends across the year |
| Channel Performance | Ticket revenue by marketing channel |
| Campaign ROAS | Advertising return by campaign |

## Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Cards
Displays total ticket revenue, advertising spend, tickets sold, and ROAS.

### 2. Monthly Revenue Analysis
A line chart shows monthly ticket revenue trends and helps identify changes in performance throughout the year.

### 3. Marketing Channel Analysis
A bar chart compares ticket revenue across channels such as social media, email, influencers, search ads, and display ads.

### 4. Campaign Performance
A doughnut chart compares ROAS across marketing campaigns.

### 5. Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard by region, channel, and target audience.

## Key Business Questions

- How much ticket revenue was generated?
- How much was spent on advertising?
- Which marketing channels generated the highest ticket revenue?
- How did ticket revenue change month by month?
- Which campaigns delivered the highest ROAS?
- How can marketing expenditure be allocated more effectively?

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate high-revenue marketing channels to understand what drives their performance.
- Evaluate campaign ROAS before reallocating advertising budgets.
- Monitor monthly revenue changes to identify seasonal patterns.
- Compare campaign performance across regions and target audiences.
- Use campaign results to improve future marketing strategies.
